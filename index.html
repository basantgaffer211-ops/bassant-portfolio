file 01 · Ahmed Hamdy Studio

Identity through
the person behind it

The brand

Ahmed Hamdy: a formal menswear brand for suits and formal wear. Luxurious, in Egypt.

Campaign overview

A creative campaign designed to build Ahmed Hamdy Studio’s identity by highlighting the person, passion and thinking behind the craft. It uses storytelling and cinematic concepts to communicate the studio’s artistic value, creative process and distinctive personality.

Strategic objective

Move beyond conventional promotional content and create a distinctive brand identity through storytelling, artistic positioning and memorable creative execution.

Ahmed Hamdy Talk 01: “The Art of Tailoring. Why Italian Soul, Egyptian Made?”

Two ideas

1 · A storytelling series exploring three dimensions of Ahmed Hamdy’s identity and work.
2 · The In Line: a cinematic hero advertisement (next page).

Creative direction

Three complementary stories that communicate one identity: the craftsman, the artist and the thinker.

The In Line:
the hero advertisement

Concept

A hero advertisement built on curiosity. It grabs attention by borrowing the language of the spy film: suspense, sophistication and a quiet chase, so it feels like a scene from a movie, not a promotion.

The big idea

The suit is the treasure, and everyone wants it.

Creative direction

Turn a promotional advertisement into an entertaining cinematic narrative that is hard to scroll past.

file 02 · Mind Solution

Digital transformation
without the headache

The brand

Mind Solution is a software company that implements Odoo (ERP) systems for companies.

The plan

A campaign concept that reflects the daily pain of small and medium businesses, then reframes the problem as a workflow problem, not a people problem.

Phase 1 · Awareness (errors and hints)

Idea: reflect daily SME pain as undefined system errors.
Core concept: “Something is wrong, but no one can name it.”
Goal: create curiosity, recognition and tension without naming the brand.

Error pop-up: “Vulnerability found. Think, it’s not illegal yet.”

Warning stack: “Your existence will now be erased.”

“Initializing…”: a process that never finishes.

Copy examples: Error 404: Clear Process Not Found. System Warning: Workflow Not Optimized.

file 03 · the concept

احنا بنحسبها…
بالسنّتي

The concept

SIYA is a contracting and interior design firm. Campaign 1, “The Precision Manifesto”, sets the “good enough” culture against SIYA’s uncompromising precision. Tagline: «احنا بنحسبها… بالسنّتي» (“We calculate it, to the cent”).

Target

A+ clients who notice details and reject compromise.

Format

A short cinematic film, 45 to 60 seconds, in three beats:

  • The old world: workers shrug off crooked tiles, an off-center tap and a messy paint edge with “no need to count it to the cent”.
  • The turn: a SIYA engineer sets the measuring tool down and says the line that changes the film.
  • The payoff: a silent final shot of the finished project in perfect harmony, ending on the SIYA line.
